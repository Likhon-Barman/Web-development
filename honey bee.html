The domestic honeybee (Apis mellifera), also frequantly spelled "honey bee" and know scientifically as the European or westren honeynbee, is the most common of the nine species of honey-producing bees. It is navit to african Europe and the Middle East.

Beekeeping, or apiculture, is the human managment of honey bee colonies.The practice dates back to almost 9000 years ago, acording to a 2015 paper publised in the jurnal Nature.Morden beekeepers use human-made bee hives that are typically boxes with row of wooden frames that bees fill with wax honeycomb. The hives are designed to give beekeepers access to collcet honey and maintain the nest
